The US Department of Homeland Security is running one of the largest border-surveillance hardware expansions in its history — and it's almost entirely autonomous.

## The numbers

DHS plans to expand its network of border surveillance towers from **830 currently deployed to 2,300 by 2034**, at a cost of roughly **$1 billion**, funded through the "One Big Beautiful Act" spending law signed in 2025. That's not a modest upgrade — it's close to a threefold increase in physical surveillance infrastructure along US borders over the next eight years.

## What's actually being built

The **Integrated Surveillance Tower (IST)** programme has three components: autonomous towers that use radar, thermal-infrared and optical sensors to track targets over long distances without a human operator watching a feed continuously; fixed towers oriented toward foot and vehicle traffic monitoring; and remote video-surveillance systems positioned near border areas. **Anduril Industries** is the most prominently featured vendor across current deployments, supplying autonomous towers including its **Sentry** system.

## The tension worth naming

This is squarely defence *and* homeland-security technology, and it comes with a genuine civil-liberties dimension that a purely capability-focused writeup would miss. The Electronic Frontier Foundation's analysis, which first surfaced the scale of this build-out, documents that these systems' surveillance range extends well past the border line itself — into residential neighbourhoods, school playgrounds and backyards in towns near the US-Mexico border — raising real questions about what "border surveillance" ends up capturing in practice.

## Why it matters

Autonomous, sensor-fused surveillance towers are a genuine defence-technology category in their own right — they use largely the same radar-and-optical-fusion approach as military ISR systems, built by the same contractor base, just pointed inward rather than at a foreign adversary. As this technology scales toward 2,300 towers, expect scrutiny of range, data retention and oversight to scale right alongside it.
