About KINETIX

Global defence technology intelligence, built on sourcing discipline.

KINETIX is an independent publication covering the technologies reshaping modern defence: land systems, naval and maritime power, air and aerospace, missiles and space, unmanned and autonomous systems, cyber and electronic warfare, artificial intelligence and C4ISR, soldier systems, homeland and border security, defence-industrial policy, and the geopolitics that sits behind all of it.

The name is built the way defence programmes themselves often are — as an acronym with a real meaning, not a decorative word: Kinetic Intelligence Network for Emerging Technology, Interception & eXploration. Each piece of that maps to something on this site: "kinetic" is the military term for physical, hard-power action; "intelligence network" is the multi-source, multi-language collection process behind every article; "interception" nods to the missile-defence and electronic-warfare coverage; "exploration" to the space and emerging-technology beat.

The coverage itself is global by design and doesn't favour any single country's narrative — we report on programmes from the United States, Europe, China, Russia, Japan, Korea, Israel, India and beyond.

What we cover, and how

Every article on KINETIX is built from publicly available material: official statements, government and defence-ministry releases, established defence-trade press, peer-reviewed research, and on-the-record statements from companies and officials. We do not publish classified, leaked, or operationally sensitive material, and we do not publish information designed to help anyone build or use a weapon.

Editorial independence

KINETIX is not affiliated with, funded by, or written on behalf of any government, ministry of defence, political party, or defence contractor. Sponsored or promotional content, if it ever appears, will always be clearly labelled as such.
