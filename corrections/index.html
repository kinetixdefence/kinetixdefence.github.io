Corrections

We'd rather fix it in public than pretend it didn't happen.

If you believe a KINETIX article contains a factual error, email corrections@kinetixdefence.example with the article link and, where possible, a source supporting the correction. Confirmed errors are corrected with a visible note and date at the top of the article.
